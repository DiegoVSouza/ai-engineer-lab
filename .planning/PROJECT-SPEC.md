# Lab specification — Independent AI project factory

## Objective

Maintain a reusable, versioned harness kit that creates independent portfolio projects with explicit Builder, Product, Shared, and Kit layers.

## Scope

- The lab owns `harness-kit/`, global operating rules, and the SDD templates.
- Each project under `projects/<name>/` owns its profile, product code, evaluations, evidence, and documentation.
- The former P00–P05 SupportOps materials remain a learning archive rather than a dependency graph.

## Constraints

- Product work begins only after project intake and its first SDD phase are documented.
- The kit is generalized only after validation in at least two real projects.
- LLM control paths support fake, replay, and live modes; live activity stays within an approved budget.
